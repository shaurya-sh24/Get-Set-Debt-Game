# Get,Set,Debt Repository

YOU NEED TO REPAY YOUR DEBT TO THE FROG MAFIA! You got greedy, and now you owe them five million dabloons. Gamble your life away trapped in a room which poorly imitates nature; well, you won't be touching grass anyway for a good while...
SPIN the roulette wheel, collect cards and make combinations that can earn your money to ensure your survival. Can you escape?


This game was made for The Very Serious Juniper Dev Game Jam.
It is our first game jam and also our very first 3D game :D

NOTE:  No GENERATIVE AI has been used at any point in the making of this game.
All game assets are made by us.
Sound effects and background audio are open source, picked from Pixabay.

If you find any bugs in the game, please reach out to us. 
(If the game breaks, please restart)
